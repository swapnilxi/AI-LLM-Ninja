# General FAQ (shared)

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Use the self-service portal at the login page and choose "Forgot password". The link expires after 30 minutes.

Q: Who approves travel expenses?
A: Your line manager approves expenses up to 1,000 EUR; above that, the finance director must also approve.

Q: What is the standard support response time?
A: One business day for normal tickets and four hours for tickets marked urgent.
